# Weibo Toolkit v0.11.0

Expands Follower Hygiene for multi-batch workflows with saved filter presets, sorting, protected-account skip rules, and a local removal log.

## Highlights

- **Bulk fill & multi-batch flow:** Added **按顺序选满** to fill up to 200 eligible accounts across pages in filter order. Confirmed removals are immediately hidden from hygiene results so subsequent batches can be selected without re-scanning first.
- **Protected accounts:** Page selection and bulk fill automatically skip mutual followings (from the snapshot) and accounts with Friend Notes; these accounts can still be ticked individually with protection reasons shown in the confirmation list.
- **Filter presets, sorting & removal log:** Save up to 8 filter presets per account, sort results by follower count, post count, or registration date (unknown values last), and record confirmed removals in a searchable local log (up to 5,000 entries, CSV exportable).
- **Progress & event grouping:** Reopen in-progress manual scans from Home, review completed background results on return, group Relationship Events into one card per account, and confirm before discarding unsaved Friend Note drafts.

## Upgrade & Verification

Update in place and refresh Weibo. Existing storage schemas and `Backup v3` compatibility are unchanged (filter presets and the removal log are browser-local preferences/logs outside backup files). Verified with build checks and 12 offline test suites.
